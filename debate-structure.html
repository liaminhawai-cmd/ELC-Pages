🎤 How a formal debate runs

Type your speakers' names down the sides. Drag a name, or use ↑ ↓, to change the order — that changes who opens and who sums up. Both teams always give the same number of arguments, so if a team has fewer people, someone speaks twice.

Team A's first speaker never rebuts

Nobody has spoken yet, so there is nothing to answer. Every speaker after that one starts with a rebuttal — that is why rebuttal is always red.

The last speaker adds no new arguments

Their job is to rebut, then pull the team case together. A new argument here is unfair — the other team has no turn left to answer it.

Teams can be different sizes

Both teams always give the same number of arguments — that is what keeps the debate fair. If a team has fewer people, someone takes two turns, and their name is marked ×2.
