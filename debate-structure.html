🎤 How a formal debate runs

Type your two team names. The order below is the order you speak in, and each bubble says what that speaker's job is.

The first speaker of Team A never rebuts

Nobody has spoken yet, so there is nothing to answer. Every speaker after that one starts with a rebuttal.

The last speaker adds no new arguments

Their job is to rebut, then pull the whole team case together. A new argument here is unfair — the other team has no turn left to answer it.

Rebut a detail, not just the topic

Name what they actually said: “The first speaker argued that… however…”. The rubric rewards answering their specific example.
