Lab & Matter

Term 3 · safety, measuring, matter

This unit is the science room itself. First the rules — what a hazard is, what the risk is, and why the safety glasses go on before anything else. Then the tools: the beaker, the measuring cylinder, the thermometer, the tongs, and the Bunsen burner with its safety flame and its heating flame. Then the measuring — read the scale, write the data with its unit, and turn the table into a line graph whose trend you can say out loud. Last, what everything is made of: the atom and its protons, neutrons and electrons, the bonds that hold them together, what dissolves in what, and where a substance sits on the pH scale.

Everything here is preparation, not permission. A practical is done in the laboratory, with your teacher watching — never alone, never at home.
