Family and home

How does Noyce use camera shots to show why family matters?

▲ Idea and theme■ Technique and evidence● Effect and purpose◆ Language and structure

C2

Name the shot

Being separated from family is painful. Noyce uses a close-up of Maude’s face as the girls are taken away. We can see how upset she is.

C3

Explain the effect

Noyce shows how painful it is for families to be separated. The close-up of Maude’s distressed face helps us understand how much the separation hurts her because we can see her expression clearly.

C4

Link two shots

Noyce shows that forced separation hurts the whole family. The close-up of Maude’s face shows her distress. The film then cuts to a long shot of the family on the ground, surrounded by open land. Together, the shots show how the removal affects the family left behind.

VCE 1

Explain the purpose

Noyce presents forced removal as a loss for the whole family. The close-up draws us towards Maude’s distress, while the long shot widens our view to include the family left behind. This shift invites the audience to understand the policies behind the Stolen Generations through the pain they caused Aboriginal families.
