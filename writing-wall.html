What the colours mean

The same three colours run through the rubric rows and the example sentences, so a highlighted phrase tells you which part of the rubric it is doing.

  • Evidence & metalanguageThe technique you name, and the quote or moment you use.
  • Purpose & interpretationThe effect it has on the reader or audience.
  • Ideas & themesThe bigger idea, message or value it points to.
