Foundations

Open all year · number fluency, units, symbols

Foundations is not a topic. It is the set of things you should never have to stop and work out: the tables up to 12, the squares and their roots, and turning one unit into another without guessing which way to divide. A speed in km/h is a rate — a ratio of two different quantities, distance over time — so changing it to m/s is two conversions, not one. The symbols matter as much as the numbers: ≤, ≥, ≠, ≈, ±, √ and π are words, and an equation is a sentence in which = means “is”. None of this is new. All of it gets faster with practice, and every other unit runs on it.
