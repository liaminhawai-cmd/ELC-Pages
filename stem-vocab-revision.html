Linear Graphs — the quiz words

A short lesson in four steps. By the end you will know what every word in the Linear Equations Progress Quiz is asking you to do.

Learning intention

We are learning to use the language of linear graphs so the quiz questions make sense.

Success criteria

    1 · Engage — which words do you already know?

    Every word here came up in the last unit. You met gradient, substitution, y-intercept and the point of intersection in the Progress Quiz. Tap a word to open your notebook: read the meaning, look at the word parts, and read it in your first language.

    Which words do you already know? Which parts look familiar? Tap at least three words before you go on.

    2 · Develop and Discover — the word parts

    Most of these words are built from Latin parts. Learn the parts once and you can read a word you have never seen before. intersection = inter (between) + sect (cut) + ion (the act of): one line cutting across another. A road intersection is the same idea. substitution = sub (under) + stitut (set up) + ion: a number set up in place of a letter.

    Read the word parts below. Words that share a part belong to the same family. Then answer the three questions under them.

    Quick check — three questions

    3 · Apply — use the words

    Ten short tasks: build words from their parts, match words to meanings, and find the maths words inside real quiz questions.

    4 · Review and Reflect

    Tick what you can do now. Be honest — this is for you.
