Before you read

A motion is a proposal that the House votes on. The Leader of the Opposition had moved a motion to remove the Speaker, Peter Slipper, because of sexist text messages Slipper had sent. The Prime Minister rose to oppose that motion. She did not defend the text messages. She argued that the man moving the motion had no standing to make it.

Julia Gillard
Prime Minister, and Australia's first woman to hold that office.
The Leader of the Opposition
Tony Abbott, who leads the other side of the House. She never uses his name.
The Speaker
Peter Slipper, "the member for Fisher", who runs the House. The motion was about him.
The Deputy Speaker
Anna Burke, chairing the House while this speech was given.

A note on two words. The speech quotes two insults that were written about the Prime Minister on protest signs. They are quoted, not said by her, and they are the evidence her argument rests on. They are in the record and they are left in.
