How to read the map

  • Rows are the strands — words, maths and science side by side, so you can see what one task pulls together.
  • A tall column is a task you will actually do. Everything to its left feeds it.
  • ✓ means every skill or word in that cell is finished. 3/4 means three of its four are done.
  • A cell can split into lanes — the maths band stacks lines & graphing above intercepts & sketching.
  • Dashed and grey means not open yet.
