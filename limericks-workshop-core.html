Limerick Builder

Learn the pattern together, practise with choices, use the guided builder, then invent your own rhyme banks and write independently.

1

I do — notice the pattern

Read the complete example aloud. Reveal one feature at a time.

There once was a young man from Ealing,
Who would always hang from the ceiling.
He couldn’t wear a hat,
But could hang like a bat,
And said, “What a wonderful feeling!”
AABBA: lines 1, 2 and 5 rhyme and are longer. Lines 3 and 4 rhyme and are shorter. The first line introduces the subject; the last line gives the consequence or joke.
