Limerick Builder

Five lines, two rhymes, a bouncy beat and a joke at the end. Pick your rhymes, build your lines, then clap-check the syllables.

1 · Read one first

Lines 1, 2 and 5 are long and rhyme together (A). Lines 3 and 4 are short and rhyme with each other (B). Tap any line to see its syllables.

2 · Pick your two rhyme families

One family for the A lines, a different one for the B lines. Tap once for A, tap a second family for B.

3 · Build it, line by line

Tap a grey stem to start the line, type the middle, tap a coloured rhyme word to end it. Then Count — tap a dot between syllables if the computer split a word wrongly. Your claps are the judge, not the computer.

4 · Finish

Read it aloud with the limerick bounce — clap the strong beats. Then read it to someone.

The habit line — who always hops, who usually sleeps, who never once stops — is the present simple doing its favourite job. More of that in the Grammar Hub, and more rhyme in the Pronunciation Hub.
