Before we begin

What is a limerick?

A limerick is a short, funny poem with five lines. It has two rhyme families and a strong bouncing rhythm.

5 linesIt is always a five-line poem.
AABBALines 1, 2 and 5 rhyme. Lines 3 and 4 rhyme.
Long, long, short, short, longThe B lines are usually shorter.
A joke or surpriseThe final line often gives the punchline.
Learning intention: We are learning how rhyme and syllables create a limerick.
Success: I can recognise AABBA, split words into syllables, plan rhyme banks and write five lines.
Warm-up · I do

Notice the pattern

Read the poem aloud first. Then reveal its features one at a time.

There once was a young man from Ealing,
Who would always hang from the ceiling.
He couldn’t wear a hat,
But could hang like a bat,
And said, “What a wonderful feeling!”
