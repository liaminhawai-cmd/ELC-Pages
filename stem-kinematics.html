Kinematics

Term 3 · motion, linear graphs, data

Kinematics is the study of movement: how far, how fast, which way. First the words — why distance is not displacement, and why velocity is speed with a direction. Then the graphs — a steady journey is a linear line, and the gradient of a position-time graph is the velocity. Last, the data — you drop a ball, plot the numbers, and find the trend yourself.
