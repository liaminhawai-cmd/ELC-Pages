Vocab hub

52 words in 9 lessons, in the order the unit is taught: initial impacts, Pemulwuy and Yagan, Myall Creek, the Frontier Wars, Victoria, and Federation. Build each word from its parts, then use it, with base camp and stretch tiers and a report to save as a PDF.

Before you start. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander readers are advised that these pages name people who have died.

The history. Some of these words name violent events: massacres, the Frontier Wars and the removal of children from their families. The notes say plainly what happened, with dates and places. Where historians’ numbers differ, the notes say so.

The translations. Each key word is given in Mandarin (Traditional and Simplified characters) and Vietnamese. The translations are drafted and not yet checked by a speaker of each language.
